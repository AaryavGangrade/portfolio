WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Double bottom / W

A potential bullish reversal after two tests of a similar support level.

01 / Two similar lows

The second bottom stays near the first, with a rebound between them.

02 / Volume context

The scanner checks for accumulation at the second low and a volume surge at breakout.

03 / Neckline breakout

Price moves above the intervening peak before the setup is considered confirmed.
